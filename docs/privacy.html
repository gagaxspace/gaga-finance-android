GaGa Finance — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026. App: GaGa Finance, Android package app.gagafinance.android. Developer: GaGa Finance / gagaxspace development project.

What the app does

GaGa Finance is an offline manual finance notebook. It records user-entered income, expenses, category budgets, savings goals, people's names and optional contact details, lending/borrowing records, loan repayment history and bill due dates. It does not offer bank accounts, real money transfers, investments, credit decisions or verified balances.

Collection, use and sharing

These records are created and managed on your device. The current offline version has no account sign-in, no server synchronisation, no developer-operated database receiving those records, and no third-party analytics or advertising SDK. The app uses stored records to display totals, reports, payment-due indicators and optional on-device notifications. Data is not sold to advertisers. You may deliberately export CSV or JSON files and save/share them using Android's document picker; files then fall under the chosen destination's privacy and security.

Security and permissions

The app keeps its SQLite records in Android's private app storage, but it does not add independent database-at-rest encryption. The device's own storage protection may apply. Do not enter banking passwords, identity-document numbers or highly sensitive credentials. Complete JSON backups created by recent versions can be encrypted with your chosen password using AES-256-GCM and a password-derived key. The password is not stored by the app and cannot be recovered. CSV exports and historical unencrypted JSON backups are not password protected. Android notification permission is optional and requested only if you choose daily bill and loan reminders. Notifications use generic counts rather than people's names or amounts on the lockscreen; reminder timing is not exact.

Retention and deletion

Records remain in app storage until you delete individual supported records, choose Settings > Reset everything (with confirmation), or uninstall/clear app data. Uninstalling may permanently erase records. If you export a file, resetting the app does not delete files you saved elsewhere; delete those separately. This version does not create online accounts, so there is no server-side account to delete.

Children and service limitations

GaGa Finance is designed for general adult personal record-keeping. It is not intended as a financial service for children. There are no connected bank, wallet, brokerage or cloud AI providers in this offline release.

Updates

Changes in features or data processing will be reflected here and in the app's instructions or release notes before distribution. This policy applies to the current offline build; later online features would require a new disclosure.

Contact

For non-sensitive questions about this policy, contact the project maintainers through GaGa Finance's issue tracker. Never post private financial information or credentials in a public issue. The developer must verify and replace this provisional contact mechanism with an appropriate private privacy contact before a public Play Store launch.
